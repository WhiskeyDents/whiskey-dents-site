The Whiskey Dents

The Whiskey Dents are a Dallas-area three-piece making loud, rough-edged, home-rolled rock ’n’ roll with plenty of indie flavor. A North Texas-rooted musical gumbo: garage heat, punk attitude, blues underneath it all, with a little surf rock and alt-country spice.

The Dents came together the way a lot of good bands do: years of friendship, shared history, and eventually finding a sound together. The songs are built the old-fashioned way: three people in a room, turning it up and hitting it till it feels right.
